Studio

A creative area that connects different disciplines and cultural pieces — where the workmanship of handmade and natural fabrics such as silk, is brought to life, to be seen and felt as part of a small collection of ready-to-wear and bespoke pieces.

We are always happy to welcome you at our studio in Düsseldorf for a personal appointment. We look to ensure a comfortable experience and can talk through any specific pieces or enquiries you had in mind. All pieces can be pre-ordered and a personalised guidance allows a chance to see through our specialised fabrics in person or to decide on a particular colour or discuss alterations.
